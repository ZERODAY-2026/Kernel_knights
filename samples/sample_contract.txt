RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. TERM AND PARTIES. This Lease Agreement is entered into between Landlord (Apex Properties LLC) and Tenant (Jane Doe) for the premises located at 123 Oak Street, Suite 4B. The term shall commence on November 1, 2026 and continue month-to-month until terminated.

2. RENT AND PAYMENT. Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,200 due on the first day of each calendar month. A late charge of $50 shall be assessed if rent is not received by the Landlord within five (5) days after the due date.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT AND PENALTIES. Tenant shall deposit $1,200 as security against damages. If Tenant breaches any condition of this lease or terminates early without sixty (60) days prior written notice, the entire security deposit shall be forfeited immediately as liquidated damages.

4. SUBLETTING AND ASSIGNMENT. Tenant shall not sublet the premises or assign this agreement in whole or in part without the prior express written consent of the Landlord. Any unauthorized sublease shall be deemed null and void and grounds for immediate eviction.

5. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this tenancy by delivering written notice at least thirty (30) days prior to the desired end date. Landlord reserves the right to terminate immediately for non-payment of rent or breach of lease covenants.

6. GOVERNING LAW. This agreement shall be governed by and construed under the laws of the State of California. Any disputes arising hereunder shall be resolved in the municipal court of the county in which the premises are situated.
